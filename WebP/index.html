Segment Player

Paste a list of video segment links or local filenames (one per line, in play order). Google Drive share links are auto-converted; plain filenames/paths are matched against a local folder you open; files ending in .gpg/.pgp/.asc are decrypted in memory. Segments are downloaded (or read) to memory and played back-to-back.

PGP-encrypted segments

Files ending in .gpg, .pgp, or .asc are treated as PGP-encrypted and decrypted in memory with the key below before playback. The key, passphrase, and decrypted video data are never written to disk, localStorage, or sent anywhere — they exist only in this tab's memory for this session, and are gone on reload or when you click Clear Key.
